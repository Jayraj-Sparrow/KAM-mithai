Questions

Is it safe for people with diabetes?

There's no added sugar, but besan is a carbohydrate, so it still affects blood sugar. If you have diabetes, treat it like any treat and check with your doctor. Our glucose study results will be published here.

Does it taste different?

That's the whole point of Spot the Sugar. Try it at a pop-up or order a box and judge for yourself.

What is the sweetener?

It's named on every box's ingredient list, with the amount per piece.

Where do you deliver?

Across India by courier. Orders over ₹999 ship free.
